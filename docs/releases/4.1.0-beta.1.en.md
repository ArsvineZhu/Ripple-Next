# Ripple Next 4.1.0-beta.1

> Status: Release preparation for PR #14. Merge and publication await the user's confirmation.

Your next action, close at hand. Ripple Next brings music, search, workflows, AI, clipboard and tasks into one floating Island, with a dedicated window for the settings that make it yours.

## A place for each action

- Seven Island pages: Browser Search, Workflows & Quick Apps, Overview, Now Playing, AI Assistant, Clipboard and Tasks.
- Settings opens in its own window with eight categories, secondary editors, automatic saving, explicit credential commits, confirmation for destructive actions, and scroll/focus restoration.
- Feature drafts survive navigation and collapse. Keyboard shortcuts, native scrolling and reduced-motion preferences keep the same operations available across input methods.

## Music that keeps up

- Windows uses native GSMTC events; Linux uses MPRIS property/session signals. Playback controls address the displayed player without a pre-action metadata query.
- Metadata appears before asynchronous local artwork. Per-session generations discard obsolete covers, including rapid A → B → A changes and reused temporary URLs.
- Idle stays shorter without a reserved playback slot. Hover quick view grows, shows playback control and always includes the artist. The idle artist preference affects idle only.
- DOM-measured long text resumes after hidden pages return. Track transitions keep one current layer. 3D artwork, intensity and scrolling are configurable in Music settings.
- Multiple players use Automatic and stable player indicators with vertical navigation; controls and player activation stay attached to the selected session.

## Native Linux observation

MPRIS and BlueZ signals, persistent PipeWire/PulseAudio streams and a bundled C inotify camera listener update music and device state through events. Healthy listeners do not periodically sample. Native-source failures trigger bounded recovery; camera hotplug and opens/closes reconcile active holders, and PipeWire video capture contributes activity.

## Make it yours

Choose Alibaba PuHuiTi 3, System or OpenRunde, a theme, colors/background, display, placement, corners, page order and behavior. Settings Liquid Glass provides clear pressed lenses, stop attraction and a live material preview.

Simplified Chinese offers Standard, Microsoft Chinese and Apple Chinese. The playful styles include contextual wording across guides, settings, empty states and music, while technical fields, critical actions, errors and regional formatting retain their ordinary meaning.

## Your tools, together

Workflows execute apps, commands and addresses in order with per-target feedback. Quick apps launch independently. AI supports OpenAI-compatible Chat Completions, OpenAI Responses and Anthropic Messages, encrypted credentials, model-specific prompts/parameters, ordered injections and streamed Markdown/code copy. Clipboard history is bounded to this run; tasks persist until completed.

The user guide and product tour include actual English screenshots and application recordings, with Chinese-specific demonstrations for Chinese expression. Recordings have selectable accompanying instructions, user-controlled playback and offscreen pause.

## Downloads and data

Release packages cover Windows x64 (MSI/portable ZIP), Linux x64 (DEB/RPM/portable ZIP), and macOS x64/arm64 (DMG/portable ZIP). macOS packages use an ad-hoc signature. Preferences, tasks, workflows and encrypted credential records live in the Ripple Next user-data directory; back up the closed application's database before moving data.

Island Liquid Glass and AI context awareness are unavailable. Windows/Linux keep the tray visible. See [usage](../../instructions.md), [platform support](../platform-compatibility.md) and [release acceptance](../release.md).
